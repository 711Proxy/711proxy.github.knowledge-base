## Common Causes of Inefficient Ad Performance


For many advertisers, the core issue on Facebook and Instagram isn't creative quality or bidding strategy, it's improper network environment configuration. Ad platforms' risk control systems continuously evaluate the trustworthiness of the operating environment.


If the IP location of the admin backend doesn't align with the target market, or if the IP itself carries a history of risk records, the health score of your ad account can be negatively affected. This may lead to abnormal ad spend, failed learning phases, and even account limitations.


## The Core Value of U.S. Residential IPs


When targeting the US market, IP "purity" is especially critical. Risk control systems on platforms like Facebook and Instagram focus heavily on an IP's source attributes, whether it belongs to a real residential broadband user or an advertiser operating from a data center.


US [residential IPs](https://www.711proxy.com/?utm_t=1&utm_i=536), directly assigned by local internet service providers, inherently carry higher trust scores in platform risk models. In contrast, data center IPs, due to their concentrated sources and low cost, are more likely to be flagged as abnormal traffic, which can compromise the long-term stability of your ad accounts.
