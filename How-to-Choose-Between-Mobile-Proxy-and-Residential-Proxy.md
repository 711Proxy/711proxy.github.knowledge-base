## What Is a Mobile Proxy?


A [mobile proxy](https://www.711proxy.com/?utm_t=1&utm_i=536) refers to IP addresses originating from real mobile devices, such as smartphones and tablets, connected to 3G, 4G, or 5G mobile networks. These IPs are assigned by mobile network operators and offer reliable connectivity.


Mobile proxy IPs change dynamically with network activity, cell tower handovers, and other factors, which contributes to their strong network credibility.


## What Is a Residential Proxy?


A residential proxy originates from real IP addresses assigned by Internet Service Providers (ISPs), complete with physical address and ISP information. Residential proxies cover a wide geographic range and typically support city-level targeting across multiple countries and regions. They are widely used in various business scenarios that require stable network environments.


## Key Differences


Mobile proxies and residential proxies differ in the following key aspects:


### Different IP Sources.

Mobile proxy IPs are assigned by mobile network operators' cellular infrastructure and originate from real mobile devices. Residential proxy IPs, in contrast, come from home broadband service providers and belong to conventional fixed broadband networks.


### Cost Variations.

Residential proxies are generally more cost-effective due to the large scale of available IP pools and relatively abundant supply. Mobile proxies, on the other hand, involve higher service costs due to the scarcity of mobile network resources and the operational complexity of cellular infrastructure. They are typically billed by traffic volume.


### Different Use Case Focus.

Residential proxies, with their broad IP distribution and stable network performance, are commonly used for data scraping, market price monitoring, and e-commerce operations support. Mobile proxies are more frequently applied in mobile ad landing page compliance verification and mobile application interface testing, scenarios that require a mobile network environment. The two types complement each other in business positioning, allowing users to combine them as needed.
